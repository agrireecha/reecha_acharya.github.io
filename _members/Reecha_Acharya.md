I am a graduate research assistant at the University of Minnesota in the Department of Agronomy and Plant Genetics. I am advised by Prof. Kevin Smith and work in the barley breeding program on both spring and winter barley projects. My research projects are focused on resistance against stresses in barley. I work on enhancing Fusarium headblight resistance in spring barley and enhancing winter survival in barley using proxy traits for germplasm screening.
